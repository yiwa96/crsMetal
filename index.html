About Us

Trusted experts with a commitment to excellence!

With over 30 years of industry experience, CRS Metal Sdn Bhd has become a trusted leader in the design, manufacturing, and installation of roller shutters, collapsible gates, and metal engineering solutions. Our decades of expertise, combined with our commitment to quality, ensures that every product we deliver meets the highest standards of durability, functionality, and safety.

We are proud to be an ISO-certified company, a testament to our unwavering commitment to maintaining top-tier quality control, safety practices, and environmental standards in all our operations. Our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to delivering solutions to meet the unique needs of our clients across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.

At CRS Metal, we believe in building long-term relationships with our customers, underpinned by confidence, reliability, and security in every project we undertake.
